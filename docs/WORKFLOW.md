# Workflow

## Foundation
`bootstrap` creates: metadata, story_bible, master_outline, cover_brief.

## Serial chapter cycle
1. chapter-start N
2. chapter plan
3. draft/revise
4. review
5. summary
6. canon / foreshadow updates
7. approve N
8. continue N+1

## Rolling planning
Keep a stable premise/ending constraint, volume goals, a 20–30 chapter rolling plan, a 3–5 chapter detailed plan, and one chapter execution plan. Do not detail hundreds of chapters at once.

## Final audit
Check unresolved foreshadowing, unresolved major characters, main conflict, hard rules, ending promises and chapter numbering before final export.
